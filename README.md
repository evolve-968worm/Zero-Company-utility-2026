## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Tactical movement consumes Action Points, and operators can move into protective cover when available. Use the Action Point option before repositioning to extend movement and preserve flexibility for attacks or abilities. Story missions may also include limited third-person free-roam sections between tactical encounters.

### Tools & Inventory

Operators can be customized with different specializations, appearances, loadouts, weapons, abilities, and equipment. Credits support purchases at The Den, while the utility's resource options can help maintain a larger range of equipment choices without normal campaign limitations.

### World & Environment

The Den is the company's base of operations on the Ring of Kafrene. From the holotable, players choose tactical missions and non-combat operations on an evolving galaxy map. Intel is used for operations that can reward items, credits, influence, and relationship progress. Complete important activities before deploying, since tactical missions advance the current campaign cycle.

### Combat & Weapons

Each operator has health and three Action Points per turn. AP can be spent on movement, ranged or melee attacks, abilities, backup attacks, overwatch, and rallying downed allies. Cover affects battlefield safety, Advantage fuels special actions, and squad bonds unlock support abilities and cross-training benefits. Use unlimited AP and Advantage selectively when testing different tactical approaches.
